# Company Overview

## Company Name

**NovaCart**

## Tagline

> _Your everyday marketplace, powered by data._

## Business Model

NovaCart is a fictional **Business-to-Consumer (B2C)** e-commerce marketplace operating across Southeast Asia. The platform connects millions of customers with third-party sellers through both web and mobile applications, offering a wide range of product categories, including **Fashion**, **Electronics**, **Home & Living**, and **Health & Beauty**.

## Business Challenge

Despite experiencing strong customer acquisition, NovaCart has observed that a large proportion of customers make only a single purchase and do not return. As customer acquisition costs continue to increase, improving customer retention has become a strategic priority. This project aims to build an end-to-end Customer Analytics Platform that enables data-driven decision-making for customer retention, segmentation, and churn prevention.
