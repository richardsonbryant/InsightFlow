# Functional Requirements

## FR-1 Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard shall provide:

- Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Interactive filters for **Date**, **Product Category**, and **City**

---

## FR-2 Customer Dashboard

The Customer Dashboard shall provide:

- New vs. Returning Customers
- Customer Retention & Churn Metrics
- Customer Segment Distribution
- Customer Detail View

---

## FR-3 Customer Segmentation

The system shall support:

- RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis
- Automatic Customer Segmentation

---

## FR-4 Churn Prediction

The system shall provide:

- Customer Churn Probability
- Customer Search
- Top-N High-Risk Customers

---

## FR-5 Prediction Explanation

The system shall explain the key factors influencing each churn prediction using business-friendly language that is easy for non-technical users to understand.

---

## FR-6 Business Report

The system shall generate business reports containing key insights along with clear and actionable recommendations.
